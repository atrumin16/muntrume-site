FAQ

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about joining Muntrume Motorsport or working with us.

"Can't find your answer? Send us a message or join our Discord — we're happy to help!"

How can I join Muntrume Motorsport as a driver?

We're always looking for talented drivers who share our passion for simracing. To apply, send us a message through the form above with your iRacing iRating, racing experience, and availability. We typically hold tryouts every few months for new positions.

What are the requirements to become a team member?

We look for drivers with a minimum iRating of 1500+, good sportsmanship ratings, and availability for team events (typically evenings/weekends CET). Most importantly, we value teamwork, commitment, and a willingness to learn and improve.

Do you offer sponsorship opportunities?

Yes! We offer various sponsorship packages for brands looking to reach the simracing community. Benefits include logo placement on our cars, social media mentions, content creation, and access to our growing audience. Contact us for our sponsorship deck.

What simracing platform do you compete on?

We primarily compete on iRacing, focusing on endurance events like the 24 Hours of Spa, 24 Hours of Daytona, and other prestigious races. Our team also participates in selected league championships throughout the year.

How quickly can I expect a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. For urgent matters, Discord is the fastest way to reach us — our team is active daily and typically responds within minutes during European evening hours.

Can I collaborate with you for content or media?

Absolutely! We love collaborating with content creators, journalists, and other teams. Whether it's joint streams, interviews, or featuring our story — reach out through the contact form or Discord and let's create something great together.
