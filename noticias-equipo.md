# Victory at 24H Spa Virtual: GT3 Class Win
**Date:** 2026-07-02
**Author:** Alberto Trujillo
**Category:** Endurance
**Image:** assets/img/race1.jpg

Muntrume Motorsport has achieved a monumental milestone in virtual endurance racing by securing an outright GT3 Class victory at the prestigious 24 Hours of Spa Virtual.

Facing a grid of over 50 competitive simracing squads and adverse Ardennes weather shifts, our squad delivered a masterclass in telemetry discipline, strategic fuel management, and consistent pace through the treacherous night stints.

### Strategic Highlights & Race Execution

- **P1 Finish in GT3 Class:** Completed 582 flawless laps with a 42-second margin over second position.
- **Fastest Race Lap:** Recorded during the cool dawn transition at 05:14 UTC.
- **Zero Major Incidents:** Zero drive-through penalties and pristine bodywork across all 24 hours of racing.

> "Our telemetry preparation leading into race week was the decisive factor. While our competitors struggled with excessive rear tire overheating during the double stints, our kinematic damper setup kept slip angles below 6 degrees throughout the race." — Alberto Trujillo, Lead Driver

The driver rotation between Alberto Trujillo, Mateo Berbis, and Eduardo Chongkan operated with surgical precision during pit stops, maximizing track position and securing our first major 24-hour endurance trophy of the season.

# High-Downforce Testing at Nürburgring Nordschleife
**Date:** 2026-06-20
**Author:** Mateo Berbis
**Category:** Testing
**Image:** assets/img/coche4.webp

Our engineering division completed a comprehensive 4-hour continuous testing simulation on the legendary Nürburgring Nordschleife to benchmark aerodynamic configurations and damper valving ahead of the upcoming VLN endurance round.

Testing focused on high-speed stability through the Flugplatz and Schwedenkreuz sections, alongside mechanical compliance over the notorious Karussell concrete banking.

### Test Benchmarks & Telemetry Ingestion

- **Telemetry Ingestion Rate:** 360Hz sensor logging covering damper velocities and spring displacement.
- **Tire Thermal Window:** Optimal operating range sustained between 82°C and 88°C on medium slick compounds.
- **Aero Map Calibration:** Low-rake aerodynamic trim validated for high-speed stability without compromising mechanical traction in slow sectors.

The data gathered during the session will form the baseline setup file for all team entries in upcoming endurance rounds.

# Le Mans Virtual Preparation: Circuit de la Sarthe Practice
**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Author:** Alberto Trujillo
**Category:** Endurance
**Image:** assets/img/coche3.webp

Preparations for the 24 Hours of Le Mans Virtual are officially underway as our endurance drivers took to the 13.6-kilometer Circuit de la Sarthe for intensive aerodynamic and fuel-trim validation.

The primary objective was calibrating low-downforce aero packages to achieve competitive straight-line velocities down the Mulsanne Straight while maintaining confidence through the demanding Porsche Curves.

### Test Focus Areas

- **Lift-and-Coast Fuel Saving:** Evaluating telemetry deltas between early throttle lift versus hard trail-braking to extend stint lengths by up to two laps.
- **Night Transition Visibility:** Cockpit FOV and virtual lighting adjustments to optimize driver vision during nocturnal stints.
- **Drafting & Slipstream Efficiency:** Multi-car telemetry testing to determine optimal spacing for clean air passing maneuvers.

With consistent long-run pace established during initial simulations, our focus now shifts toward driver stint handoff rehearsals and qualifying setup refinement.
