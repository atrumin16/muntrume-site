// THE GENESIS

Born From A Dream, Built With Precision

Every team begins with an obsession. Ours began with the 24 Hours of Spa virtual race and the desire to build a serious simracing division grounded in data science and Catalan pride.

"We didn't just want to turn laps; we wanted to understand why every hundredth of a second was won or lost on the kerbs."

— Alberto Trujillo, Founder & Lead Driver

01 // SUMMER 2024 · THE DREAM IS BORN

First Laps & Endurance Ambition

Alberto began preparing for the virtual 24 Hours of Spa. Recognizing that victory in top split endurance requires more than raw pace, the foundational blueprint for Muntrume Motorsport was formulated in Barcelona.

02 // LATE 2024 · SQUAD INCORPORATION

Official Team Launch in Sant Andreu

Adopting the name Muntrume Motorsport, the squad unified its branding under the MRT-269 banner. Telemetry data pipelines were established to log suspension travel and tire surface degradation during live sessions.

03 // 2025 · ROSTER EXPANSION

Engineering & Telemetry Integration

Drivers Mateo Berbis and Eduardo Chongkan joined the cockpit. Eduardo assumed race engineering responsibilities, introducing mathematical damper histograms and aero balance calculations that propelled the team to 20 victories.

04 // 2026 · PRESENT ERA

Multi-Class Campaign & AI Race Engineering

With Juanlu Carrillo joining the developmental squad and the rollout of the autonomous AI Race Engineer setup generator, Muntrume enters the 2026 calendar competing in GT3, GTP, Formula and Cup series worldwide.
