HISTORY

Our Story

From a summer dream in Barcelona to a competitive simracing team. Every milestone tells a piece of who we are.

"Every great team starts with a single dream. Ours began with the 24 Hours of Spa."

— Alberto, Founder

ORIGIN Summer 2024

The Dream is Born

In the summer of 2024, Alberto dreamed of competing in the legendary 24 Hours of Spa. What started as a personal ambition quickly evolved into something bigger — a vision to create a simracing team rooted in Catalan values and technical excellence.

FOUNDATION Late 2024

Muntrume Motorsport is Founded

From Sant Andreu, Barcelona, the team officially took shape. The name Muntrume became a symbol of our identity — a fusion of passion, heritage, and the relentless pursuit of performance on virtual tracks around the world.

GROWTH 2025

Building the Team

Drivers, engineers, and strategists joined the mission. We established our presence on iRacing, began competing in endurance events, and started sharing our journey with the simracing community through our YouTube channel.

TODAY 2026

Competing at the Highest Level

Today, Muntrume Motorsport is a competitive force in simracing. With cutting-edge AI-assisted race engineering, a growing community, and the same dream that started it all — we're just getting started.
