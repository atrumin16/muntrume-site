The Genesis

Born From Passion, Engineered For Precision

Every team begins with an obsession. Ours began with the virtual 24 Hours of Spa and the desire to build a serious sim racing division grounded in data science, rigorous preparation, and pride in our roots.

"We didn't just want to turn laps; we wanted to understand why every hundredth of a second was won or lost on the kerbs."

Summer 2024 · Inception

First Laps & Endurance Ambition

Alberto began preparing for the virtual 24 Hours of Spa. Recognizing that victory in top-split endurance requires more than raw pace, the foundational blueprint for Muntrume Motorsport was formulated in Barcelona.

Late 2024 · Squad Incorporation

Official Team Launch in Sant Andreu

Adopting the name Muntrume Motorsport, the squad unified its identity. Telemetry data pipelines were established to log suspension travel, damper histograms, and tire thermal degradation during live sessions.

2025 · Roster Expansion

Engineering & Telemetry Integration

Drivers Mateo Berbis and Eduardo Chongkan joined the cockpit. Eduardo assumed race engineering responsibilities, introducing mathematical kinematic setups and aero balance calculations that propelled the team to 20 victories.

2026 · Present Campaign

Multi-Class Campaign & AI Race Engineering

With Juanlu Carrillo joining the developmental squad and the rollout of the autonomous AI Race Setup Assistant, Muntrume enters the 2026 calendar competing across GT3, GTP Prototypes, Single-Seater, and Mazda MX-5 series worldwide.

Competitive Pillars

What Drives Our Squad

The three non-negotiable principles behind every qualifying lap, pit window execution, and engineering debrief.

01

Telemetry-Driven Precision

Lap time is not made by intuition alone. We log slip angles, brake line pressure, suspension velocity, and yaw rates to build setups suited for grueling 24-hour stints.

02

Catalan Grit & Identity

Proudly representing Sant Andreu, Barcelona on international virtual podiums. Passion, technical pride, and unwavering perseverance under pressure define our racing culture.

03

Endurance Team Synergy

Individual hotlaps do not win 24-hour races. Flawless driver swaps, consistent pace, tire management, and disciplined spotter communication win endurance championships.
